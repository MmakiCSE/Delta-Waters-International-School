About us

Our story

Delta Waters opened in 1993 as a secondary and boarding school with 90 students. It later added a primary section and now serves hundreds of learners across both.

The school is in Boseja Ward, Maun, next to the Wildlife Training Institute, in the town known as the gateway to the Okavango Delta.
